# Valwood opening prologue

Run [Launch_Valwood_Opening.sh](/home/computer/Desktop/final_engine/Launch_Valwood_Opening.sh), then choose **Begin the Journey**. The journey starts **house → household bills → console → New Game** and carries the father into the Meadows of Valois. The father comes home exhausted from work and struggling with everyday costs; choosing escape through a video game starts the journey.

## First arrival: a campsite in the meadows

The **v16 arrival redesign is active**. The route is **home → bills → console New Game → campsite and three first contacts → waystone**. The launcher uses the frozen V7 runtime with V7 terrain and art-v3. The current Windows r8 update contains the same reviewed revision; r7 and r6 remain historical releases.

The father arrives in a small woodland clearing beside a lit cooking fire with a chicken roasting over it. The terrain now continues beyond the previous hard edge, the stray water band is removed, and the western ridge has a varied silhouette and additional woodland layers. Irregular soil and grass shoulders replace the broad bare clearing. Canvas, timber, bedrolls and cooking props have been repaired; three distinct existing character assets stand at the first-contact positions. Three travelers are his first contacts:

- **Rowan, camp cook:** offers food and safety, and names the Meadows of Valois.
- **Bram, roadwarden:** explains the waystone route and points him toward Mara at the town tavern.
- **Ellis, traveling herbalist:** helps him steady himself while he tries to understand the transport and find a way home.

The conversations stay personal and brief. The father is an ordinary, confused parent; this meeting does not give him armor or establish him as a knight. Each traveler can be approached in any order. The objective counts completed introductions from 0/3 to 3/3, then points to the waystone. Walking around the clearing and along the paths remains available before all three introductions are complete.

Press **E or A/Cross** to start or advance a conversation; release and press again for every line. The on-screen Continue button also works. **Escape or B/Circle** leaves the conversation without crediting an unfinished introduction. Movement and look pause during the stable conversation shot; the shot moves only the render camera, not the father's playable position.

Completed conversations save as a three-person progress mask with the normal checkpoint and room-light states. Loading or retrying preserves completed introductions and closes any partial conversation; an unfinished exchange starts from its beginning on the next interaction. This visual upgrade **does not reset the owner’s introductions**. The living-world save and graphics preferences are preserved separately. **Continue Checkpoint** resumes that retained progress. ## Supplied example scene rebuild

The active opening recreates the purchased Finward **House Props & Furniture Vol. 2** demo arrangement in the native game. This supersedes the earlier pass that only fitted selected furnishings into our original apartment. The full display scene spans approximately 49.5 × 12 metres and contains 701 source prefab assemblies. Fifteen composed near/mid model pairs render the house and new story additions; the original 15 furniture assemblies / 30 near/mid GLBs remain available separately for editor import.

The source's four HDR lightmaps are sampled through the original lightmap UVs, transforms and directional maps. Movable doors, drawers and other unlightmapped pieces use exact source Unity light-probe samples. Interior reflections use room-bounded captures without the outdoor sky fallback. Original geometry, PBR textures and materials are retained. The missing-material background plane from the publisher's source scene is omitted; it is not part of the playable house floor.

The four-shot, sixteen-second opening frames the furnished room, household bills, television and playable handoff. The cinematic polish tightens the room and television framing, makes the bill notice upright and readable in its focused insert, and shortens the final settle to 2.5 seconds with a forward-facing approach to the player's exact standing view. A restrained private interior grade lifts furniture midtones and trims the eight source window emitters; source geometry and baked textures remain intact. The bills, existing console, animated television and four physical room switches are authored additions. The source arrangement is retained around them. Hold the skip control to enter gameplay sooner.

The current grass pass increases source grass tip swing by approximately 2.3× and small grass amplitude by 1.67×, with fixed roots and unchanged source bend limits. Matching temporal confidence keeps moving blades clear. The current game, environment editors and active playtest shader copies include this tuning. A fixed-camera native before/after review passed 41 captures per version; see the [grass review](/home/computer/Desktop/final_engine/new_engine/docs/grass-wind/2026-11-04/FINAL_REVIEW.json). Choose **Continue Checkpoint** to resume the preserved story checkpoint.

The current city and meadow finish corrects the imported RGB paint decoder, softens ground relief, calibrates dry soil, blends path shoulders and the town entrance, and seals tiny terrain gaps. Grass uses varied clump shapes and four refined contact placements while retaining the stronger wind. Neutral lighting and a weaker private color grade replace the orange soil and lavender streets. These changes are installed in the city editor project, city playtest and active opening. Earlier scene banks are frozen so their materials and saves remain intact.

Read the bills on the small table, approach the console beside the television and interact. Release the interaction button before confirming **NEW GAME** with Enter or A/Cross. Escape or B/Circle steps away from the menu. Transport moves toward the TV and leads into the meadow reveal. Speak to the three travelers at the campsite, then follow the path to the waystone. The front door does not trigger travel.

## Controls and checkpoints

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move / look | WASD / mouse | Left / right stick |
| Sprint | Left Shift | LB/L1 or left-stick click |
| Jump | Space | X/Square |
| Interact / room switch | E while facing the target | A/Cross |
| Confirm TV New Game | Enter or click | A/Cross after releasing it |
| Cancel TV menu | Escape | B/Circle |
| Advance camp dialogue | Fresh E press or Continue | Fresh A/Cross press |
| Leave camp dialogue | Escape | B/Circle |
| Pause | Escape | Start/Options |
| Journal | J or Tab | Y/Triangle |
| Skip cinematic | Hold Enter or Space | Hold A/Cross |
| Graphics / display | F10 or pause menu | Pause menu |

Pause also includes sensitivity, deadzone, invert Y and reduced motion. Living room, kitchen, bedroom and bathroom each have a working wall switch. Their states are saved with story checkpoints. The source bake combines daylight and fixture illumination: switches approximate 30% retained daylight plus 70% electric bounce, while native fixture highlights use 10% of source intensity. This is not a separately baked daylight/electric lighting system.

F5 saves, F8 restores and R retries the supported authored checkpoint during gameplay. The new arrangement uses a separate private project.
