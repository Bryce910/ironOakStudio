VALWOOD — PLAYABLE OPENING / LINUX

Use the root Launch_Valwood_Demo.sh for the apartment-to-Seldonne demo.
Launch_Valwood_Editor.sh, Launch_Valwood_Arena.sh, Launch_Valwood_Joust.sh and
Launch_Valwood_World.sh use this same Linux binary and shared shader bank.
One native game/editor session at a time. Existing progress and drafts remain
in their original locations. Demo, World and Editor share valwood_current.

This generation adds lossless pose GPU paging (96 MiB target working set),
with exact A/B endpoints prepared before drawing. Large active draws may
exceed that budget to retain all their endpoints. Unused vertex storage is
reclaimed while buffer names, UVs, indices, materials and animation timing
stay unchanged. A private temporary backing file closes at the last owner.
Disk failure falls back to ordinary eager loading at the loading boundary.

Synchronous animation-bank loads reuse material resolutions only after an
exact source-byte and request match. Every hit retains its ordinary image
reference; model frees and the end of the loading scope clear the borrowed
lookup. No material data or quality tier is changed.

The matched opening benchmark retains all combat and swim banks. Tracked
allocation totals are logical GL storage, not measured driver VRAM residency.
Normal saved Balanced settings keep the prior adaptive resolution behavior.
This is not a locked 60 FPS claim or an AAA completion claim. Scenery,
pale terrain slopes, faces and close-up presentation still need art work.

Review reports and actual game captures are under review/. The intermediate
pager-only-prototype is archived evidence and is not the published result.
No Windows build was prepared in this generation.
