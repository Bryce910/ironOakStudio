Valwood opening performance and ground polish - 18 November 2026

The normal Linux launchers use one runtime for Demo, Editor, Arena, Joust and
World. The existing renderer passes, lighting, shaders, source textures,
foliage density, combat timing and controls are preserved.

This release removes unused baked-light floats from pose vertex buffers when
those poses have no baked-light stream. All positions, normals, UVs, tangents
and packed source paint stay exact. Identical immutable triangle-index buffers
are shared with explicit lifetime ownership. The native GPU regression covers
13 real pose pairs, synthetic geometry, five forward/reverse blend weights,
invalid-state containment, texture ownership and shared-buffer release.

Matched 1600x900 full-resolution opening views improved from19-24FPS to30-37FPS,
with frame times reduced36-41%. The sum of tracked peak GPU allocations fell
from3728MiB to3090MiB: about638MiB saved. This is logical allocation tracking,
not a driver VRAM-residency measurement.

The ordinary World-exploration opening idle view averaged43.59FPS, versus38.49
before, on the same RX5300 and saved Balanced settings. Automatic rendering
reached1120x630 in a1600x900 window. Its1%low was38.93FPS. This idle check does
not certify sustained movement/combat performance. It is not a locked60FPS,
and legacy animation-bank startup remains slow.

An extra forest depth pass was tested and rejected because it had mixed
results between enclosed woodland and open approaches. Its experimental
source/binary/reports remain under review/prepass-prototype; they are not the
shipping implementation or the source of the reported final measurements.

The existing arrival leaf beds have broader soft boundaries and a warmer
woodland palette. Soil coverage extends along the first bend. No new image,
material layer, paint stamp or render pass was added. Grass masks retain16free
slots for the designer. Pale exposed rock faces and coarse distant terrain
still need further art work; the scene is not claimed to be AAA-finished.

Terrain shape, rivers, collision, placements, NPC routes, dialogue, quests and
saved progress are preserved. Native human/Orc sword-contact, first/third-person
poses, controller jousting and shared editor/game shader-lifecycle checks
passed. The full editor UI and complete quest playthrough were not rerun in
this storage-only pass. All104 application units have verified dependency
receipts, with unchanged objects reused and the model unit rebuilt.

No Windows build or new asset purchase was performed.
