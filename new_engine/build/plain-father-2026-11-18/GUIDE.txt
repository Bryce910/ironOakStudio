A new journey starts in third person. V or right-stick click switches view.
Your camera choice is kept through transport, checkpoint load and retry.

and accepted combat skeleton. The sword loan does not grant plate armor.

removing it returns to plain clothes, at a safe loading boundary.
Arena character selection remains independent.

through CURRENT_VALWOOD.json. This generation contains Linux work only.
