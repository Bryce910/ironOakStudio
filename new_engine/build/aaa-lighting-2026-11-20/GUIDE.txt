AAA lighting candidate, built on camp-atmosphere-2026-11-20 (2026-11-20).
Nothing published was modified; CURRENT_VALWOOD.json still points at camp-atmosphere.

Launch:  Launch_Valwood_AAA_Lighting_Candidate.sh [demo|editor|arena|joust|world]
Check:   python3 -B check_candidate.py      Rebuild: python3 -B build_candidate.py
Runs on private project copies (world-project, arena-project, joust-project,
area-projects); saves made here do not reach projects/valwood_current.

CHANGES
1. Bloom mip chain (engine/world_bloom.cpp, shaders/world_bloom_down|up.fs.glsl)
   Quarter-res soft-threshold extract, then up to 6 levels of 13-tap downsample
   and additive 3x3 tent upsample (Jimenez 2014). Wide soft halos (~250px) around
   fire, lamps and sun; threshold unchanged, so ordinary surfaces get no haze.
   Strength .03 (was .035 on one narrow level). Override: VALWOOD_BLOOM_STRENGTH.
   The old world_bloom_blur.fs.glsl is no longer used.
2. Auto exposure (engine/world_auto_exposure.cpp/.h, shaders/world_exposure_meter.fs.glsl)
   64x64 centre-weighted log-luminance meter, mip-reduced, read back via a PBO
   ring with fences (no GPU stall). Multiplies the authored exposure, which stays
   the studio "Exposure" control. Neutral band log2 -5.6..-2.3 covers every
   authored view measured (apartment, forest camp, city, castle, rainforest), so
   those keep their tuned look; darker/brighter views are 65% compensated within
   -1..+1.5 EV. Adapts faster to bright (2.6/s) than to dark (1.1/s).
   Env: VALWOOD_AUTO_EXPOSURE=0, _KEY_LOW, _KEY_HIGH, _STRENGTH, _MIN_EV, _MAX_EV.
3. Hue-stable tone mapping (shaders/world_post.fs.glsl): AgX inset/outset
   matrices around the existing curve. A/B: #define VALWOOD_HUE_STABLE_TONEMAP 0.
4. Output dither (world_post.fs.glsl): triangular +-1 LSB, removes 8-bit banding.

5. Player motion vectors for temporal AA (engine/world_object_motion.cpp/.h,
   shaders/world_object_motion.vs|fs.glsl, world_temporal.fs.glsl/.cpp, skeletal hero)
   After the scene, the skeletal player hero is redrawn into an RGBA16F motion
   buffer against the scene depth: each vertex skinned with this and last
   frame's joint palette (the renderer's double-buffered bank) plus root
   translation/yaw. TAA adds this per-object offset to camera reprojection and
   lets moving actors keep normal history. Measured on a running hero
   (forest camp, VALWOOD_QA_AUTOWALK): hero pixels with rejected history
   83% -> 1%; background unchanged. Other actors (NPCs, arena opponents,
   joust rider) still use camera-only reprojection.
   Env: VALWOOD_OBJECT_MOTION=0 disables. VALWOOD_QA_AUTOWALK=1 (capture runs
   only) holds forward after frame 120 for verification.

6. Far sun shadows to 1 km (engine/world_viewer.cpp FarShadow; world_terrain/world_foliage .fs)
   Cached 2048^2 terrain+tree+building cascade beyond the 160 m map, refreshed after
   80 m of travel, a sun turn or 10 s; each refresh is split over three frames into a
   back buffer (~2 ms/frame; one ~12 ms build at load). Distant hills and tree lines
   now cast and receive sun shadows. The far lookup runs only past 125 m.
   Env: VALWOOD_FAR_SHADOWS=0, VALWOOD_FAR_SHADOW_RADIUS=300..4000.
7. Fog light shafts (world_weather_fog.fs.glsl): 10 near + 4 far steps, per-frame
   rotated noise integrated by TAA. Subtle in the tuned camp look.
8. Creek caustics (world_water.fs.glsl): animated sun caustics on the visible bed of
   shallow creeks/pools within 60 m, drifting downstream; brightness-neutral.
9. Meadow (world_terrain.fs.glsl, world_foliage.fs.glsl): open-meadow colour drifts at
   ~30 m and ~120 m (lush green / dry straw; woodland and rainforest untouched);
   ground-cover normals lean 55% toward up so grass tufts light like the turf.
10. Creek and river bank dressing (world-project/scenery/creek_dressing_instances.txt,
   generated by tools/creek_dressing.py from the world.studio ribbons): reeds, tall
   grass, ferns (woodland creeks) and partly buried rocks; ~6.4k placed. The loader
   skips generated roots that land in water/on trails instead of failing
   (engine/world_scenery.cpp); authored files stay strict. Re-run the tool after
   moving a creek. Disable: VALWOOD_QA_DISABLE_CREEK_DRESSING=1.
   Total scenery records 15929 of 16384.
12. Upscaling (world_post.fs.glsl): below display size the scene is reconstructed with a
   5-tap Catmull-Rom and sharpened in proportion to the scale (near-native detail at
   0.75 vs blurry bilinear). This build's graphics.settings now allow auto quality down
   to min_render_scale=0.75 (was 1): forest camp 16.9 -> 21.7 fps, creek 26.4 -> 31.9.
   Set it back to 1 in Graphics settings for native-only rendering.
13. Distant relief (world_terrain.fs.glsl): past 180 m the ~100 m/texel far terrain gets a
   two-octave slope field (~140 m swales, ~45 m gullies) so broad facets break up.
14. Forest camp (round 3): Rowan, Bram and Ellis now stand at the camp in World mode too
   (they were demo-only; VALWOOD_QA_NO_CAMP_TRAVELERS=1 hides them). Hearth smoke plume
   strengthened in world-project/world.studio (particle line; rises ~9.5 m into the
   canopy). Camp props ring (logs, sacks, small barrels, chest, bucket, rope, bedrolls)
   and ~100 understorey plants, plus verge grass along the arrival trail, are in the
   generated dressing file. Ecology: camp clearing grass .25->.55, ferns .04->.18;
   woodland ferns .7->.95; meadow grass .85->.95, ferns .32->.45; cover caps ferns
   360->1100, rocks 180->420 (engine/world_cover.h). Generated short grass keeps ~85% of
   blades (was 50%; VALWOOD_GRASS_KEEP=0..1). Camp at auto 0.75 scale: 21.7 -> 19.7 fps.
11. Foliage depth pre-pass: measured 2.7 ms SLOWER, now opt-in (VALWOOD_FOLIAGE_PREPASS=1).

PERFORMANCE (RX 5300, 1600x900 Ultra, forest camp): published 59.0 ms GPU (16.8 fps);
candidate 58.6 ms with everything on. Creek view: +3.3 ms for bank dressing.
Foliage shading is ~31 of those ms (alpha-test only: 5 ms); every lighting feature
costs 3-7 ms (register pressure), so the next real speed-up is temporal upscaling.

NOTE: the hard vertical light/shade line on a far hill in the region-meadow view is a real
ridge seen end-on in the coarse far heightmap (mirror-image face normals), not a tile bug.
NOT DONE: GTAO (cost), NPC/opponent motion vectors (needs a shared instance-layout change),
true TAA upscaling (resolve at display size); the post upscaler above is the interim.

VERIFIED (AMD RX 5300, 1600x900): demo, world (camp, rainforest-camp, city-walk,
castle) and arena start and render with no shader/GL errors; meter readbacks
178/180 frames with zero skips; forcing the band moved exposure -0.61 EV as
expected. Joust and arena also start cleanly (2026-11-20). Demo peak memory 2572 MB vs
2589 MB published. Not verified: the editor UI, GPU frame-time cost.
