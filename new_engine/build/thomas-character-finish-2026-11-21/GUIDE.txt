Thomas third-person character finish — Linux Valwood, 21 November 2026

Launch with the usual Valwood launcher after publication; restart an already
running game to load this generation. World, Demo and Editor share the same
third-person protagonist, owned motion bank and saved character/model choice.

In F10 > Quality > Characters / models, choose Character detail: Low, Medium,
High or Ultra. Apply and restart. This changes the character mesh and texture
resolution together. Your saved choice is preserved. Global graphics presets
and automatic scene-resolution adjustment leave this manual choice independent.
Third person is the default; V retains the camera-view switch.

Tier       Civilian triangles   Armored triangles
Low                    64,703             60,221
Medium                 80,640             73,557
High                  114,168            103,337
Ultra                 114,168            103,337

Low and Medium retain reduced garment geometry and smaller textures. Every tier
retains the original animated face, eyelids and hair topology. High retains the
full original mesh; Ultra adds original color detail up to 4K within the texture
budget. Thomas keeps the same face when story armor equips.

The face now uses fifteen purchased authored controls, including natural blinks,
small independent gaze shifts and restrained exertion brow movement. Blinks use
the authored downward-eye correction for complete lid closure. Camera orbit does
not make him stare into the camera. Expressions hold during pause and reset with
the character; defeat closes the eyes. This is ambient facial animation, not a
recorded dialogue performance or lip sync. There is no unsupported mouth opening.

Grounded, stowed-weapon exploration now uses twenty-two purchased Kubold start,
stop and turning takes. Direction changes from idle select angled starts; running
reversals use moving pivots. Small deliberate analog directional input can turn
in place. Camera orbit remains independent. Captured displacement is requested
before the normal collision sweeps, and only accepted travel advances the pose.
Short taps, obstacles, pause and interruptions cancel or blend the motion without
storing movement to replay later. Armed combat, swimming, jumping, defeat and
equipment presentation retain their existing motion paths.

Thomas hair now has a dedicated source-based fiber response, authored tangent
and normal treatment, corrected alpha coverage and backlight response. The
selected material preserves brunette albedo/detail with the purchased tint and
uses the HDRP fiber response; it is an intentional hybrid rather than an exact
Unity HDRP render. Other actor materials retain their existing response. The
previous bounded temporal coverage smoothing is retained.

Close-up source hair-card clumps, missing skeletal motion reprojection, Unity
cloth/hair simulation and complete HDRP skin/eye parity remain limitations. This
pass does not certify the complete game as AAA. Original licensed Unity packages,
user saves, settings and world authoring data are preserved. Windows is unchanged.

The owned source and shader banks are compiled as a verified 104-unit native
closure. Evidence and the practical limits of testing are in review/FINAL_REVIEW.json.
