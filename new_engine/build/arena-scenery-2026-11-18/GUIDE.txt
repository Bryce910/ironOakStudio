Valwood — woodland tournament arena scenery pass, 18 November 2026

Use the normal desktop Launch_Valwood_Arena.sh to play.
The arena has packed PBR earth, a roofed timber spectator stand, a framed
entrance, timber boundary cladding, layered planting and leaf-litter ground.
The 24 x 24 m flat fighting floor and all existing barriers are retained.
The stand and gate are decorative, outside the playable ring.

One Linux executable and one shared shader/character texture bank serve
Demo, Editor, Arena, Joust and World. The environment dressing in this
pass applies to the combat arena; other areas keep their existing art.
No Windows build was produced.

Graphics settings and progress are retained. Existing 4K character quality
remains optional; the measured scenery comparison uses Balanced, 1600x900,
Medium foliage distance, High density and Medium character textures.

New free materials: Brown Mud and Wood Planks from Poly Haven (CC0).
https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud
https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks
Existing licensed vegetation and props are reused. The custom stand,
entrance and boundary are authored for Valwood. Provenance and hashes
are in downloads/PROVENANCE.json. No purchase is needed for this pass.

Review files include matched native screenshots, six no-readback camera
benchmarks, combat/equipment checks, scope and scene audits. Inherited
animation and control checks are verified through the previous generation;
they are not described as newly rerun. Camera benchmarks do not establish
a sustained frame-rate guarantee for 24-opponent battles.
