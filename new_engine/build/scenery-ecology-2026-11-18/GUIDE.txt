Valwood scenery ecology — 18 November2026

Normal desktop launchers use this generation. The binary and shaders are the
same verified Linux renderer as the preceding arena scenery release.

This pass authors persistent canopy-aware vegetation in the woodland arena
and the opening campsite/road corridor. Dense crowns have fewer grass clumps,
shorter surviving grass, ferns and exposed leaf beds; sunny gaps keep fuller
patches. Trunk clearances and the existing walking routes remain clear.
This is authored vegetation distribution, not simulated plant growth.

The arena reuses owned whole-clump grass in the previous footprint. The
opening reuses an existing grass family with identical close geometry and
textures, retains rooted interactive wind, and removes individual grass
shadow draws. Its two outdated distant grass meshes are replaced by the
matching owned49-triangle MID meshes. The existing12tuft sites are retained.

The campsite uses dry Poly Haven Brown Mud color/normal maps, constant matte
roughness and warmer leaf litter.14 images/8layers remain inside the existing
16image/8layer budget. The opening keeps256 paint strokes by reshaping8
existing leaf islands. Fourteen new opening grass masks leave16mask slots for the designer.
No new lights, shader passes or frame-time canopy sampling were introduced.

The terrain, rivers, collision data, trees/buildings, NPC routes, dialogue,
quests and progress remain intact. Edited source lists contain no persistent
source-index bindings. The scene audit verifies protected records unchanged.

Private native reviews include matched views, no-readback static timings,
and the opening with the full character/presentation systems enabled. These
camera routes do not replace a complete movement or combat rehearsal.
The inherited combat, camera and control suites were verified through the
previous release and are not described as newly rerun.

Material source: https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud (CC0); original download
hashes/licensing remain in the prior arena generation'sdownloads/PROVENANCE.json.
Owned vegetation sources and prepared mesh hashes are recorded locally.
No new asset purchase or Windows build was performed.
