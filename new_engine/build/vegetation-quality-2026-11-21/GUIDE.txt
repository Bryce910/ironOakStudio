Quality-scaled vegetation — Linux Valwood, 21 November 2026

F10 > Quality > Vegetation > Grass and undergrowth detail: Low, Medium, High,
Ultra. Apply updates actual grass/fern generation and drawing without restart.
Foliage detail distance remains an independent setting. Character detail retains
its existing Low/Medium/High/Ultra control and restart behavior.

Low: up to 23,000 grass roots / 360 ferns, 35% short-blade retention.
Medium: 46,000 / 900, 50% retention.
High: 69,000 / 1,800, 75% retention.
Ultra: 92,000 / 3,600, 95% retention.
These are budgets, not guaranteed plant counts: ecology, dry-ground/path rules,
view culling, species and distance still determine visible plants.
Trees and solid procedural rocks retain the same roots at every quality level.
All rock variants can represent the full shared root pool; no quality setting
changes physical obstacles. Grass transitions support 184,000 overlapping roots
through bounded 4,096-instance draw batches, with matched fern generation/draw
budgets. Lower tiers reduce active detail, not every preloaded scratch allocation.

The authored scenery ceiling expands from 16,384 to 65,536 records. Per-alias
4,096-instance guards remain. Source files expand from ten to sixteen. Legacy
editor IDs migrate without changing the existing canonical save representation;
expanded source references use version 15. Duplicate validation uses a spatial
index instead of scanning every preceding placement.

An immutable optional creek-bank/camp vegetation overlay joins the normal main
world source transaction. It is inherited from the separate lighting candidate
and scales through existing authored vegetation density selection. Generated
bank records that cannot satisfy dry-ground/trail/burial rules are skipped;
other authored files remain strict. The user's saved world is not replaced.
Other area projects, arena and joust do not receive the main-world overlay.

This generation retains the accepted Thomas animation, facial work, corrected
hair and complete material/shader bank. The separate candidate's exposure,
bloom, far-shadow, fog-shaft and caustic changes are not part of this update.

CPU and native evidence, actual review scope and measured limitations are in
review/. Higher vegetation settings can be expensive. This update does not
certify AAA quality or sustained performance across all scenes and machines.

VERIFIED NATIVE SCOPE
Four matched quality tiers and a complete Low -> Medium -> High -> Ultra -> Low
Apply cycle, including finished regeneration/fades. Low returns to 23,000 grass
roots and 360 ferns. The sampled camp has 55,856 eligible grass roots at High
and Ultra, showing that capacity does not force plants onto unsuitable terrain.
Actual high-detail main-world third-person and Ultra creek frames render on the
AMD RX 5300 with GL error 0 and no failed/pending critical streamed assets.
The main world loads 16,023 effective authored instances including the overlay
and two existing editor props. Static capture timings remain bounded measurements.
The existing two shore texture symlinks were replaced with byte-identical local
copies so they satisfy surface-library validation and stop forcing recovery UI.
Saved world documents, profiles and settings were not replaced by private QA.
Close creek foliage can obscure the fixed inspection camera; no camera-fade
feature is added. The darker, denser canopy still needs lighting/art refinement.
