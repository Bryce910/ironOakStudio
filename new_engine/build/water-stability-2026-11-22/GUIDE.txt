VALWOOD — WATER STABILITY (Linux, 22 November 2026)

Fixes alternate-frame reflection flashing by retaining the captured mirrored
projection together with the matching reflection image. A genuinely stationary
camera can reuse the previous image for one frame. Real movement, camera turns,
FOV/aspect/target/plane changes, temporal cuts, disabled demand and failed draws
refresh or invalidate the image. The tiny floating-origin correction uses the
mirrored vertical direction. Reflection quality remains selectable Low–Ultra.

This is a focused native renderer fix. Existing camp props, character motion,
quest state, water geometry and shaders are preserved. No Windows work.
Native consecutive-frame and motion review reports are under review/.
Baseline remaining scenery, performance, optional thunder audio and bridge-post
movement limitations are not claimed fixed by this change.
