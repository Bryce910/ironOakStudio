Thomas character polish — Linux Valwood, 21 November 2026

Launch with the usual Valwood launcher after publication; restart an already
running game to load this generation. World, Demo and Editor share the same
third-person protagonist, motion bank and saved character/model preference.

In F10 > Quality > Characters / models, choose Character detail: Low, Medium,
High or Ultra. Apply and restart. This changes the main character's geometry
and texture resolution together. Existing saves retain their prior selection;
profiles without a selection use Medium. Global graphics presets preserve this
independent choice. The Loaded line shows the model tier actually resident,
even if a different preference has just been applied for the next launch.
Third person remains the default; V retains the existing camera-view switch.
The quality tier is a manual preference, so choose the detail your computer
can run comfortably. Automatic scene-resolution adjustment stays independent.

Low and Medium use reduced model and texture detail while preserving the
character's identity, hair and material boundaries. High uses the full original
character mesh with detailed textures. Ultra retains that full original mesh
and adds finer original color maps up to 4K, with surface maps kept within the
existing budget. Smaller source maps remain native, and hair keeps sufficient
coverage detail on every tier. Existing skin, mesh and crowded-roster texture
budgets remain enforced. Thomas keeps the same face when story armor equips.

Hair topology is retained at every tier; Low keeps its coverage map at 1K.

The purchased Kubold idle/walk/run bank and existing weapon, swim, defeat and
equipment clips are retained. Grounded idle support now eases the bounded
pelvis adjustment through stops and starts, avoiding the previous 3 cm snap
on a grade. Eye surface roughness follows the purchased HDRP material scalar.
Thomas hair now receives bounded temporal coverage smoothing to reduce
idle and slow-camera shimmer. Other actor materials retain their existing
response. Fast movement still lacks dedicated skeletal motion reprojection.
The engine uses its own eye shading; this does not reproduce Unity HDRP’s
complete eye shader. Facial morph animation and Unity cloth physics are not
implemented. Saves, settings files and world authoring data
are preserved. Original licensed Unity packages are unchanged.

The quality source and shader banks are owned copies and the native executable
is rebuilt from a verified 104-unit closure. Windows is unchanged. Validation
and the practical limits of the visual review are recorded in review/FINAL_REVIEW.json.
