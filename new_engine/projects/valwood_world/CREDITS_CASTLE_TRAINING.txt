Castle west-court practice animation
===================================
Animated Defender AKA Brigand.
Concept: Konstantin Maystrenko. Model: AnthonyMyers.
Retopology, rigging, source animation: Danimal.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/animated-defender-aka-brigand

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Valwood modifications: supplied Attack-two handed2 frames33..113 evaluated
offline; four authored quaternion recovery poses close the drill; root motion
removed; soles grounded; fixed source tessellation before skinning; source
poleaxe rigidly retargeted to the evaluated two-hand grip line to remove
mocap wrist roll; fixed1.82m reference scale; legacy PBR material adaptation.
Twenty-four static poses blend on the GPU. Material image bytes and sampler
policies are identical to the existing attributed Defender resident assets.
The master of arms uses four existing resident breathing poses.

The original Blender source and CC BY3.0 notice remain unchanged under
assets/characters/incoming/danimal_defender/.
No embedded source scripts are executed or included in the runtime assets.
