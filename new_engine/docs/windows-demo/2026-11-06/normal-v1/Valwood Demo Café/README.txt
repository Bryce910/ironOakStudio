LEGENDS OF VALWOOD
A Place at the Fire — Windows Demo

START
Extract the entire ZIP to a writable folder, such as your Desktop.
Double-click "Valwood Demo.exe". Keep bin and project beside the executable.
Unity, the editor and Python are not needed to play.
Choose BEGIN THE JOURNEY to start at the apartment.
The first load prepares textures and lighting; allow it to finish.

THE DEMO
An exhausted father returns home, reads his bills and starts a video game.
Follow the TV/console marker and select NEW GAME. In Valois, meet Rowan,
Bram and Ellis, borrow a sword, recover the supplies and reach town.
Allow about 10–15 minutes for a first playthrough.

KEYBOARD / MOUSE
WASD: move. Mouse: look. Shift: sprint. Space: jump or combat dodge.
E: interact and advance dialogue. Release between interactions.
Choose NEW GAME on the television with Enter or the visible button.
Left mouse: attack; select direction with mouse or arrow keys.
Right mouse: hold directional block. V: first/third person after the loan.
Esc: pause or leave a conversation. J: journal. F5/F8: save/load checkpoint.

CONTROLLER
Left stick: move. Right stick: look while exploring; directional swings in combat.
Hold LT and move the right stick to block in the matching direction.
A: interact/confirm. B: cancel/dodge. X: jump. LB: sprint.
Right-stick click: first/third person after receiving the sword.
Menu/Start: pause. Y: journal. Center sticks after menus or focus changes.

SAVES AND SETTINGS
This demo starts with fresh saves, separate from the development project.
Progress is stored in project\saves. Graphics and display options are in Pause.
The initial Balanced settings can be raised on a faster computer.
After defeat, choose Retry Encounter. Continue Checkpoint resumes a saved game.

SYSTEM
64-bit Windows and a graphics driver supporting OpenGL 3.3 are required.
Update the graphics driver if the game reports that it cannot create a context.

This is the opening-chapter demo, ending at the town checkpoint.
Third-party runtime and asset notices are supplied with the package.
