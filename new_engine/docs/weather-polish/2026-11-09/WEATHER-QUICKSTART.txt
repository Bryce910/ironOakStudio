Open Edit Seldonne Region, then choose Weather in the editor.

Global baseline: enable Override existing world weather, then choose Clear,
Overcast, Rain or Storm. Rain, Thunder and Lightning can be switched separately.
Clouds, mist and variation contains the minimum/maximum ranges and timing.

Local weather: choose Place weather area on map, then click the ground.
The inner ring has full effect. The outer ring blends into the surroundings.
Higher priority areas override lower priority areas; a clear area can shelter
a region from a global storm. Move, duplicate, Undo/Redo and Save All use the
ordinary editor workflow. Weather edits do not rebuild terrain.

Rain now has separate water, mud and stone contact responses, with drips from
nearby roof edges and leaves. Wet ground
builds surface film gradually and stays damp after rain stops. It dries over
minutes of active play. Wetness history is a bounded visual effect for the
current session; it is not saved as a fluid simulation.

Storm clouds, cloud shadows and lighting use the authored weather areas.
Lightning illuminates nearby exposed surfaces and clouds. Thunder is delayed
by distance, changes recording with distance and is muffled indoors.

Save All saves weather settings and areas. A paused, unfocused or loading game
does not advance weather time or catch up missed thunder when resumed.
