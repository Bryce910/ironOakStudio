# Seldonne riverbank and woodland detail

This pass adds 21 dry-bank and creekhead contours and 24 paired soil/gravel transitions. It recomposes 1,100 existing plants, including 48 shrubs, plus 40 rocks and 36 trees around the western riverbank, southern terrace, central confluence, eastern tributary and northern rocky pockets.

The region still has 8,076 authored scenery placements, including 1,363 trees, 6,184 low plants and 317 rocks. It uses the existing model and texture families. The campsite, town supports and gardens, roads, bridge decks, water level and river topology are preserved. Plants and rocks are seated using their transformed visible mesh bases.

Use the workspace **Play_Seldonne_Region.sh** and **Edit_Seldonne_Region.sh** shortcuts after installation. **F11** switches play/editor, **Tab** opens the atlas, and **V** changes the camera. The corner minimap follows the player. **F9** cycles 18 scene viewpoints: `city-meadow`, `city-walk` and `city-woodland` support walking; the other viewpoints are inspection cameras.

The new `city-woodland` walking view starts on the existing western woodland path, facing the river gallery. The eight `detail-` views inspect creekheads, shore planting, the confluence and rocky pockets. The ordinary Play shortcut continues to start at the arrival campsite.

The designer workcopy owns its control files, texture cache and import folders. Existing library files are linked. Import a new asset revision rather than editing a linked library file in place. The closed source snapshot and QA copies are evidence, not the designer workcopy.

All 16 road/river records and 256 terrain paint strokes are in use. Adjust existing splines and paint records before extending these authoring limits. This pass adds environment detail; building interiors and new gameplay content remain separate work. Windows visual checks use Wine; this pass does not replace earlier standalone Windows demo archives.
