# Valdoa city and countryside — playable slice

Run Launch_Playtest.bat in this NEW installed playtest folder. No Unity or Python installation is needed. Keep the console available during first loading. Graphics settings are under F10; start with Balanced/Performance on a small GPU and raise quality on stronger hardware.

## Route

Follow the market stalls, west town edge, meadow and countryside, then return. The reviewed loop is about 853 metres. Allow 5–8 minutes with stops. The hilltop castle remains a visual backdrop; the original quest/vendor positions are not relocated into this purchased city.

## Controls

WASD walks; hold right mouse to look. Shift sprints, Space jumps, V switches camera. R restarts the current route/section. Esc opens pause. F10 opens graphics settings. Gold diamonds guide the route; the small map shows the path and your position. You can pause and look around along the way.

Controller: left stick walks, right stick looks, LB/L1 or left-stick click sprints, X/Square jumps, right-stick click switches camera, Start/Options pauses. A/Cross uses Grandstone's travel prompt. Pause-menu navigation uses the left stick/D-pad and confirm/back buttons.

F5 saves this playtest session; F8 restores its position, camera, route progress and section. Pause offers the same controls. Saves are local to this playtest folder. Loading a changed or incompatible route is rejected. R restarts the current section; it does not overwrite a manual save.

## What to check

Walk slowly through transitions, sprint a stretch, switch first/third person beside walls, try jump/headroom, pause/resume, then save and reload. Look for gaps, floating props, shiny ground, clipping, invisible obstacles and slow frames. Keep the area name, nearest landmark, screenshot, graphics preset/GPU and logs/playtest-latest.log with each issue.

These are dedicated playtest projects. Use the original orientation workspace for art editing. Their launcher opens gameplay with authoring and unrelated travel shortcuts guarded. The city and Grandstone slices focus on traversal and visual review; new vendors, quests, enemies and rewards have not been added to these routes.
