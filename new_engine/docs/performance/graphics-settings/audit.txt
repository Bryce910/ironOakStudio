Observed baseline evidence
--------------------------
The existing docs/performance/wildmesh/native/*.json reports are observations
from prior native runs, not measurements of proposed settings or adaptation:
  farm:       58.83 mean FPS, p99 20.902 ms, max21.681 ms, 0 >22.222 ms;
              GPU16.360 / shadow1.870 / main foliage6.022 / reflection2.482 ms.
  market:     59.95 mean FPS, p99 16.735 ms, max16.911 ms, 0 >22.222 ms;
              GPU16.275 / shadow3.147 / main foliage7.103 / reflection0 ms.
  woodland:   59.95 mean FPS, p99 16.734 ms, max16.873 ms, 0 >22.222 ms;
              GPU12.610 / shadow1.302 / main foliage6.182 / reflection0 ms.
  rainforest: 55.48 mean FPS, p99 22.176 ms, max22.921 ms, 13 >22.222 ms;
              GPU16.069 / shadow2.617 / main foliage6.513 / reflection1.130 ms.
GPU values are delayed snapshots, not exact attribution of individual misses.
Woodland mean raw_editor13.277 ms with GPU12.610 ms /59.95 FPS demonstrates
that editor-side submission/backpressure timing cannot be treated as pure CPU
work. A CPU/GPU bottleneck classification must not sum CPU and GPU timings:
their pipelines overlap. Isolated present/editor stalls are not evidence that
the image should be permanently downscaled.

Existing integration hooks and actual available controls
-------------------------------------------------------
1. world_viewer.cpp Target::resize (~248): HDR color8 + depth nominal4 +
   indirect8 bytes per internal pixel. Currently target dimensions equal the
   drawable. Separating render dimensions from drawable dimensions is the
   largest broadly useful control: terrain/foliage fragment cost, water copies,
   TAA, AO, weather, main sky and reflections then scale with pixel count.
   90% linear scale has81% pixels;85% has72.25%;75% has56.25%.
   This does not reduce shadow raster size, triangles/draw calls, simulation,
   image residency, decode/upload stalls, or all CPU-side selection costs.

2. Shadow::init (~650): defaults2048, but castle/wildlife scenic launch uses
   3072; VALWOOD_SHADOW_RESOLUTION permits2048/3072/4096. This launch-prefix
   difference should become explicit preset state, consistent after travel.
   Current light_program (~910) always forces uSoftShadows=1. All three
   material shaders already support a4-tap alternative to9-tap filtering.
   Shadow proxy meshes are implemented and should stay enabled in every
   ordinary preset; disabling them increases cost without better main color.
   Screen-resolution reduction cannot reduce shadow-map raster cost.

3. Reflection (~2571): independently allocated, half of INTERNAL dimensions,
   floor320x180, refreshed every ready visible-water frame. A preset can vary
   this scale without removing reflected subjects or temporal correctness.
   Keep main, sea, pond and creek mirror culling unions intact. Do not silently
   omit fauna/architecture/trees to meet a frame target. Skipping alternate
   reflection frames is a larger correctness/temporal change than lower mirror
   resolution and needs explicit reprojection/held-camera treatment.

4. world_sky.cpp (~131): two cached half-resolution targets,12 view steps and
   2 light samples in fixed GLSL. Main cloud cost in these reports is only
   ~0.17-0.23 ms, so sacrificing the sky first will not recover a multi-ms miss.
   Quality-step controls require actual shader/module APIs; they are not
   currently runtime fields despite stats exposing12/2.

5. world_occlusion.cpp: half-resolution16-tap AO and8 contact-ray steps,
   two edge-aware blur passes, full HDR compose. Cost ~0.86-1.00 ms here.
   AO must continue to attenuate the indirect attachment only; contact rays
   attenuate direct light. Reducing its working resolution or sample count is
   a quality option, not permission to multiply AO over final direct+fog.
   Changing AO tap count must also change its normalized sample positions;
   changing contact count must retain the same physical ray length.

6. Weather: half-resolution fog + full-resolution composition/particles;
   fixed8 ray steps, bounded8 terrain rows/update, stable terrain cache.
   Keep physical rain/wind/time and water response identical across presets.
   Artistic intensity sliders are not GPU-quality settings. Existing disable
   environment variables are QA toggles, not a coherent user preset system.

7. Terrain64 cells/tile, procedural foliage caps/distances and authored scenery
   have intentional coverage/LOD overlaps. Do not change streaming topology,
   collision, protected root selection, paths, animal counts or animation time
   as an automatic response to a low FPS sample. Fixed CPU-bound scenes need
   profiling/selection work or explicit view-distance tiers; DRS alone cannot
   solve them. Texture sampling bias/anisotropy does not free image residency.

Dynamic render-resolution correctness requirements
-------------------------------------------------
Current viewer conflates drawable and Target dimensions in several places.
Keep two independent dimension pairs:
  drawable: final default-FBO viewport, native UI/display size, screenshots,
            default-framebuffer readback and window aspect;
  internal: scene target viewport, jitter in pixel units, TAA/color/depth size,
            water capture, AO/weather frame dimensions, foliage uPixelScale,
            mirror target sizing and post input texel size.
The final pass (~2802) must fill the DRAWABLE viewport even if its scene input
is smaller. post.uResolution currently defines input neighbor texel offsets;
keep it equal to internal resolution unless adding a separate output uniform.
Full-screen normalized UVs already permit a simple upscale. This is spatial
upscaling of the existing internal-resolution TAA, not temporal superresolution.
The passive ImGui frame (~2846), loading-window center and capture sequences
currently use target.w/h: use drawable dimensions there. Retain drawable
aspect in Camera/projection; bucket rounding must not change horizontal FOV.
Set jitter NDC from internal width/height consistently in terrain, sky, water,
AO/weather and TAA. Reflections and directional shadows stay unjittered.

Apply a scale change only before a fresh ready scene, never on a held loading
image. A held image's old color/depth textures and old TAA matrices must stay
valid. Set temporal_cut/reset on dimension or quality discontinuity. Reset
query/controller history separately. Current Target, TAA, AO and weather
resizers release old targets first; DRS adds allocation failure and churn
paths. Do not leave latest_color pointing to a deleted texture. Failed changes
must restore a valid prior configuration or stop safely; no partial dimensions.
Commit only a discrete bucket when it changes; no allocation each frame.
Do not repeatedly allocate all resolution buckets on a3GB card. Main+TAA+AO+
weather+water+reflection+sky targets together are roughly83.5 bytes/internal
pixel when all paths have been allocated (~114.7 MiB at1600x900), excluding
shadow targets and persistent scene assets. DRS helps this modest transient
budget, not the much larger full-resolution imported image pool.

Timing/controller design (proposed initial thresholds; requires native tuning)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
WorldQueries (~336) is a4-slot nonblocking timestamp ring. It currently runs
only when launch->profile is set and returns just a retained milliseconds
value. Normal adaptive play needs always-on TOTAL timing, with explicit fresh
sample serial, source rendered frame and configuration epoch in each slot.
Consume each completed sample once. Poll availability; never GL_QUERY_RESULT
on an unavailable slot, never glFinish. Discard old-epoch samples after scale
or preset changes and suppress stale data. Existing ring iteration is slot
order, not guaranteed source-frame order, so order completed samples by frame
or consume aggregates without treating their slot order as consecutive time.
The main interval begins before shadow and ends after post; excludes UI/present.

Controller state should distinguish user ceiling (preset/manual render scale)
from effective adaptive state. A lower GPU workload can recover only toward
the user's ceiling. Fixed mode must never adjust. Persist intent, not a
temporary emergency downgrade as the next launch's permanent user choice.

Suggested first policy:
  -Target60 FPS =16.667 ms; desired GPU headroom14.5-15.0 ms.45 FPS floor is
   22.222 ms, not an average-FPS target.
  -Discrete linear scale buckets1.00,.95,.90,.85,.80,.75,.70; a user-visible
   minimum setting may differ. No hidden reduction beyond that bound.
  -EWMA over~0.5 s valid samples, plus fixed-size60-fresh-sample p90/runs.
   Use real elapsed time/sample timestamps for cooldowns, not clamped dt.
  -Down one bucket on >=0.5 s EWMA>16.2 ms, or8 consecutive fresh>17 ms.
   Emergency down one/two buckets after3 consecutive fresh>21 ms, bounded by
   minimum. Do not punish an isolated long raw editor/present sample.
  -Upscale only after5 s EWMA<12.5 ms and p90<14 ms; >=8 s since last reduction.
   Clear promotion counters on a heavy sample. Keep at least1 s between normal
   downward commits, with separately bounded emergency handling.
  -Warm120 ready rendered samples after startup; exclude held/loading frames,
   wildlife commit/decode/upload, minimized/unfocused/zero drawable, resize,
   teleport/camera cuts, settings application and invalid timestamps. After a
   resize/config epoch wait30 fresh rendered samples before normal decisions;
   a real sustained >22ms emergency may be allowed sooner by explicit policy.
  -Do not feed cheap held-image total GPU samples into an upscale decision.
   Do not use viewer smoothed fps (94/6%) or clamped dt for control.
  -Use CPU update timing as a diagnostic guard, not editor/present cost as a
   CPU-work estimator. If GPU remains low while repeated total frame cost is
   high, report CPU/system limited; avoid repeatedly lowering image quality.
   If GPU query timing is unavailable, hold a conservative preset and expose
   telemetry; do not invent fresh GPU samples from the last known value.

Reasonable explicit preset ladder
---------------------------------
Keep PBR materials, source geometry, foliage coverage, animation/collision and
lighting direction unchanged. Example starting points, not measured guarantees:
  Performance: shadow2048/4tap, mirror~.33, main ceiling1.0/adaptive min.70;
  Balanced:    shadow2048/9tap, mirror.50, main ceiling1.0/adaptive min.75;
  High:        shadow3072/9tap, mirror.50, main ceiling1.0/adaptive min.80;
  Cinematic:   shadow4096/9tap, mirror.75-1.0, native main, adaptation opt-in.
Keep current clouds/contact/weather present by default; optional higher sample
quality can be exposed only when its shader settings are actually implemented.
Better hardware can select larger reflection/shadow targets and native/higher
display resolution. Avoid claiming ray tracing, GI, volumetric fog quality
tiers or temporal superresolution that these shaders do not implement.

Meaningful tests against actual controller/integration
----------------------------------------------------
Pure controller tests should execute its real shared implementation:
 -Fresh-vs-stale query serials; missing, zero, NaN/Inf, out-of-order and old
  epoch samples; ring wrapping; no state change on each rejected sample.
 -Steady12ms climbs slowly to ceiling; sustained19ms drops;3x23ms emergency;
  isolated100ms CPU/present stall with healthy GPU does not reduce scale.
 -Alternating thresholds do not flap; manual ceiling/minimum and fixed mode
  honored; downward/upward cooldowns use monotonic time; behavior remains
  equivalent for45/60/120Hz sample cadence and duplicated/no-query frames.
 -Loading/resize/focus/teleport intervals cannot accumulate promotion credit;
  cheap held images cannot upscale; config epoch rejects late old queries.
 -CPU/system-limited trace does not drain every quality tier; diagnostics
  distinguish unmet floor from quality control having reached its minimum.

Offscreen GL integration (root controls actual GPU scheduling):
 -Change odd drawable/internal dimensions and all scale buckets; full drawable
  output/no black strip; unchanged FOV/UI coordinates; coherent color/depth;
  finite AO/weather/water and history reset; no sample of deleted textures.
 -Hold a prior view while destination loads, request a settings change, resume
  without reallocating the held input or mixing old/new matrices.
 -Repeated stationary frames allocate nothing; only bucket transitions resize;
  allocation failure path leaves a valid state and cleanup returns owners0.
 -Same input path under manual fixed and adaptive-disabled is identical.

Native acceptance must include representative market/creek/rainforest/wildlife
and walking/turning scenes with adaptive decisions logged (scale, preset epoch,
fresh GPU, decision reason, transition count). Report actual rendered size and
min/median scale alongside FPS. Include ordinary resize/scale-transition costs
in user-experience frame statistics even when excluded from controller voting;
do not hide adaptation stalls. Compare a fixed-quality baseline separately.
60-target /45-floor is a controllable rendering budget goal; system/present or
source asset residency stalls need separate evidence and cannot be eliminated
by a resolution controller alone.
