LEGENDS OF VALWOOD - A PLACE AT THE FIRE
Windows playtest | 10 November 2026

Begin in the apartment, start NEW GAME on the television, and arrive at the
travelers' campfire. Meet all three travelers, ask Bram for a weapon, recover
the stolen provisions, and follow Rowan's lead into town. This chapter ends
with Hugh at the Copper Lantern; Mara is your next lead.

START AND CONTROLS
Extract the entire ZIP, then open Valwood Demo.exe. Keep bin and project
beside it. Unity and the world editor are not required.

Keyboard: WASD/mouse, E interact, Left Shift sprint, Space jump/dodge.
LMB attacks; RMB guards; mouse/arrows choose combat direction.
Z stows/draws the weapon; V changes camera after the loan; I opens inventory.
Controller: right-stick cuts; LT + direction guards; X jumps while exploring
and attacks during combat. RT also attacks. B dodges during combat.
D-pad Down stows/draws; RS click changes camera; View opens inventory.

Pause > GRAPHICS AND DISPLAY offers Performance, Balanced, Cinematic and
Custom presets, plus Low/Medium/High/Ultra foliage detail distance.
The packaged default is Balanced with automatic resolution adjustment.
