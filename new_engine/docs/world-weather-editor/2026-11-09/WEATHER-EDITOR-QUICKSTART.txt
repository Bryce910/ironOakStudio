Valwood — Weather authoring

Use Edit_Seldonne_Region.sh (the Seldonne region editor shortcut), then choose
Weather from the tool palette. Other area shortcuts retain their prior editor
version for now.

Global baseline
Enable “Override existing world weather”, then choose Clear, Overcast, Rain,
or Storm. Rain, Thunder, and Lightning can each be enabled independently.
Use the minimum/maximum fields for rain intensity, storm interval in seconds,
lightning strength, and thunder volume. Expand “Clouds, mist and variation”
for cloud coverage, ground mist, variation speed, and a repeatable seed.
Transition controls how quickly applied weather changes blend.

Local weather
Choose “Place weather area on map…” and click terrain. The inner radius is
full effect; the outer radius blends to the surrounding weather. Higher
priority overrides lower priority. Move/frame/copy/duplicate/delete areas
with ordinary selection tools; Undo/Redo uses the shared editor history.
The map supports one global baseline and up to sixteen local weather areas.

Audio
Placed sounds can play Always, during Rain, when Dry, or during a Storm.
Their rain minimum/maximum values limit activation. Conditions are evaluated
at the sound source, so a distant storm does not silence a dry campsite.
Existing sound fades control the audible transition. Audition previews the
chosen clip independently of its weather condition.

Save All stores weather and audio with this map. Reopen the same project to
continue editing. Weather-only Apply does not rebuild terrain or collision.
Fog still follows the player's volumetric-fog graphics setting. Roof cover
suppresses rain particles and attenuates outdoor audio. Thunder is delayed by
distance from the visible strike; pause, focus loss and loading do not queue
missed strikes for playback on return.
