# Valwood cinematic opening r1 — Windows

Run **Launch_Opening.bat** in this independent installed seed. Choose **Begin the Journey** to watch the new apartment entrance. **Continue Checkpoint** resumes the saved playable step and bypasses the entrance film.

The father comes home exhausted, overworked and still struggling to afford ordinary life. Four authored shots establish his work belongings, overdue bills, television and apartment before settling into the playable camera. Read the bills on the coffee table, then use the console beneath the TV. Select **NEW GAME** to trigger the transport into the Meadows of Valois. The front door does not trigger it. Follow the meadow path to the waystone to finish this opening preview.

## Controls

- WASD moves; mouse looks; Left Shift sprints; Space jumps; E interacts.
- Controller: left stick moves, right stick looks, LB/L1 or left-stick click sprints, X/Square jumps and A/Cross interacts.
- At the TV menu, Enter or A/Cross starts New Game. Escape or B/Circle steps away while retaining bills progress. Release interaction before confirming.
- Hold Enter/Space or A/Cross to skip a cinematic. Held input cannot skip successive scenes or activate an interaction on handoff.
- Escape or Start/Options pauses. J/Tab or Y/Triangle opens the journal, including the inspected bills.
- F10 or pause opens graphics/display settings. Pause also has sensitivity, deadzone, invert Y and reduced motion. Reduced motion holds stable per-shot compositions during the entrance.
- Release held sticks after menus/cinematics before moving again.

## Apartment and progress

The living room, kitchen, bedroom and bathroom have independent light switches. Aim at one and press E/A when prompted. Window daylight and television power remain independent. The television begins in standby, displays the game menu when activated, and changes to transport effects after New Game.

F5 saves the supported story checkpoint; F8 restores it; R retries during gameplay. Pause provides the same actions. Checkpoints retain completed story steps and room-light states and restore authored safe positions. They never resume an interrupted film camera or a locked game menu. Begin the Journey resets the opening and lights.

The installed seed begins without saved progress and owns independent graphics settings and a project/saves folder. It does not share writable saves with artist workspaces. Keep earlier installed folders and source assets.

## Scope and receiving-PC check

This is an authored cinematic presentation around the existing playable story. It uses inner-thought subtitles, current furniture and existing audio resources. The bathroom door remains parked open and fixtures are decorative. Replacement father/hand animations, recorded dialogue, armor progression and a first combat encounter remain separate work.

The apartment opening remains first person; later third-person gameplay uses the main game's camera and character systems. These authored shots do not remove that support.

CPU package verification checks exact hashes, native dependencies and portable paths. Run the installed build on the receiving Windows PC to check graphics, audio and the physical controller. This release does not certify a frame-rate floor or final AAA art quality. If startup fails, keep logs/opening-latest.log. OpenGL 3.3 driver support is required; Unity is not.
