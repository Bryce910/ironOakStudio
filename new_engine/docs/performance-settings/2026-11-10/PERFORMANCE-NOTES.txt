Valwood demo — graphics and performance update, 10 November 2026

Press F10 in the updated opening demo to open Graphics and display.

Quality
- Grass and undergrowth amount: Low, Medium, High, Ultra. Amount and distance
  are independent. Small decorative plants are thinned by a stable seed;
  trees, rocks, buildings and movement collision stay in place.
- Foliage detail distance: Low, Medium, High, Ultra.
- Performance reduces grass, shadow resolution, reflections and volumetric
  effects. Balanced retains richer scenery. Cinematic extends detail farther
  and uses higher-resolution shadows and reflections for faster GPUs.
- Automatic image quality adjusts image resolution within the selected range.
  It respects the frame limit and a known VSync refresh rate. CPU-bound misses
  do not trigger an ineffective resolution reduction.
- Apply and save commits the selected settings. Undo edits restores the active
  settings. Recommended selects Balanced while retaining display, frame-limit,
  automatic-quality and adaptive-target preferences.

Display and frame rate
- Windowed or borderless fullscreen, supported window sizes, VSync.
- Frame-rate limit: Off, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 144 FPS.
- Use a cap that matches or evenly divides the display refresh with VSync.
  A cap is a ceiling, not a guarantee that a scene reaches that frame rate.
- The software cap yields the CPU rather than busy-spinning. It does not add
  another delay when active VSync already provides the same or a lower limit.

Existing version 1–3 graphics files keep their prior settings, full vegetation
amount and no cap. Apply a preset explicitly to choose the new quality/cost
tradeoff. Opening art, story routes and the shared player progress are kept.

The grass optimization uses full detail close to the player and lighter meshes
farther away for the two upgraded grass variants. Materials and rooted wind
remain matched. Both representations overlap with a complementary fade.

See the adjacent RESULT reports for actual measurements and their resolution,
camera, sample count and limitations. Short fixed-view samples do not establish
sustained whole-demo performance or native Windows/controller certification.
