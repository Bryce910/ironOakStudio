VALWOOD — VISIBLE EQUIPMENT AND SHARED PROGRESSION

I / controller View-Back-Share: open inventory.
Mouse, D-pad or left stick: select an item. X: equip/use.
U / controller Y: remove selected armor. B / Esc: close.
Return sticks/triggers to neutral after closing.

BODY: travel harness over civilian clothes, or the green-and-gold mail outfit.
HEAD: iron helmet, or the current character's exposed head.
HANDS: equipped leather gloves, or bare hands.
FEET: equipped travel boots, or civilian shoes/leg wraps.
The same equipment selection renders in third person, first-person arms,
shadow views and the story swimming bank. No gear loads during an equip.

One player profile now owns your items, selected equipment and money.
Arena victories earn 10 copper per opponent. Only complete victories pay.
The town merchant and arena quartermaster sell provisions and armor.
Open inventory during arena Setup/Results, or beside the town greeters, to shop:
mail 45c; helmet 20c; gloves 6c; boots 8c; bread 3c; apple 2c; draught 5c.
100 copper = 1 silver; 100 silver = 1 gold.

The arena's winnings and owned melee equipment carry into the story, and
story rewards/purchases carry back to the arena. The bow remains practice-only.
Loading or restarting a checkpoint keeps owned equipment, consumed items,
purchases and spent coins. Quest tokens follow the current story checkpoint.
Starting the story again keeps the same demo character's earned belongings;
Rowan's 12-copper reward and the raider's loot cannot be farmed by replaying.

Old story and arena saves are imported once. Their original files are kept.
Your current profile is shared-profile/player.progress beside the two projects.
Keep the shared-profile folder when moving the updated projects together.
