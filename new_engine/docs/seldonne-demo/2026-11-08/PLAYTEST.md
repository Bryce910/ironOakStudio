# Seldonne connected demo

The apartment opening now transports the player into the accepted Seldonne first region. The opening, bill inspection, television menu, arrival cinematic, all three travelers, sword loan, road encounter, provision recovery, camp reward, and town greeting form one playable chapter. The town endpoint is in Seldonne's market. Latest region terrain, source architecture, rivers, bridge collision, mud/stone riverbeds, authored swimming, foliage settings and corner minimap are retained.

Run `/home/computer/Desktop/final_engine/Launch_Valwood_Opening.sh` or `/home/computer/Desktop/final_engine/Play_Seldonne_Demo.sh`. Choose **Begin the Journey** for a fresh start. **Continue Checkpoint** uses only this new demo's save. Earlier demo/region saves and projects are preserved; their map coordinates are not imported into this chapter.

WASD/mouse move and look. E interacts. Escape pauses. V switches first/third person once the sword is available. F5 saves and F8 restores. F10 opens graphics settings, including foliage detail distance. In deep water use C/Ctrl to dive and Space to rise. Controller movement, conversations and directional sword/guard input use the existing gameplay controls and on-screen prompts.

Route: inspect bills → play the console → choose New Game → speak to Rowan, Bram and Ellis → ask Bram for the sword → follow the objective along the road → confront the raider → recover the satchel → return to Rowan → reach the town greeter. Defeat offers encounter retry without losing the conversations or sword. The earned reward is 12 coins, food and reputation, committed once.

The region and older chapter are separate preserved inputs. This demo is a new combined runtime/project. Asset-only files are linked locally; mutable controls, saves and texture cache are owned by the demo. This Linux project is not a portable Windows distribution. A Windows candidate executable is compiled separately; the previously distributed Windows archive is not changed by this local promotion.
