VALWOOD PLAYER INVENTORY

Open / close: I (keyboard), Back / View / Share (controller).
Select a tile: mouse click, or navigate with D-pad / left stick and A / Enter.
Equip a weapon / armor, or use food: button in item details, X (keyboard / controller).
Unequip selected armor: U (keyboard), Y / Triangle (controller).
Categories: click All / Gear / Food / Quest, or LB / RB.
Close: Escape or B / Circle; return all sticks, triggers and movement keys to neutral.

Story: Bram's loan grants the four existing melee loadouts and basic armor.
After defeating the road raider, approach and press E / A to search the body.
Loot contains roadwarden mail, an iron helmet, a healing draught and two apples.
Rowan's recovered supplies occupy a protected quest slot until the hand-in.
Rowan's reward adds bread and a letter; the quest still owns its original copper reward.
Bread restores 30 health; apples restore 35 stamina; draughts restore 60 health.
Items are kept when the relevant resource is full. Dead characters cannot consume food.
At the town greeters, open inventory to buy provisions with earned copper.
One hundred copper = one silver. One hundred silver = one gold.

Weapon choices change the visible weapon in first and third person.
Armor slots change protection stats. The current knight's armor mesh remains fixed;
separate compatible armor meshes are needed for different visible armor outfits.
The bow and its supplied arrows are loaned for arena practice only.
Arena equipment and currency are saved separately from the story.

Gear changes, consumed items, loot receipts and purchases save automatically.
Old story/arena saves are migrated on their next save, with no coin reset.
