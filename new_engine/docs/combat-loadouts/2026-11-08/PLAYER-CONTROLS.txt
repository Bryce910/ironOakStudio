Weapon choices
1: Two-handed sword — balanced directional cuts and thrusts.
2: Bow — hold/release RT or left mouse; LT steadies aim (arena).
3: Sword and shield — one-handed cuts; lower shield block stamina cost.
4: Two-handed axe — heavy edged strikes; slower recovery and higher stamina cost.
5: Quarterstaff — longer reach; faster blunt attacks; no decapitation.

Arena: choose a Starting weapon before Start fight, switch with 1–5 or controller D-pad left/right, or equip from Pause. Selecting equipment costs no coins.
Story: after Bram unlocks weapons, choose 1/3/4/5 or cycle with D-pad left/right. Bow practice remains in the arena.

All melee choices: right-stick flicks select directional strikes; LT + right stick selects guard. Center then flick again for up to three strikes. Release guard to recover stamina. Finish a committed attack before switching equipment.
Z / D-pad Down: put weapon away or draw. V / right-stick click: first/third person. B / Space: dodge. RB / middle mouse: arena target lock. LB / Q: arena camera look.

Equipment and animations
The owned knight's original one-handed sword/shield rig is used for the shield loadout. The axe uses the existing CC0 Poly Haven wooden_axe_02 with its original PBR maps and UVs, fitted to a1.48m two-handed haft. The quarterstaff is authored and fitted to the existing two-handed captures and posed grip. PBR images are shared by the renderer. New banks are prepared at startup, with no asset allocation or target relinking when switching weapons.
The quarterstaff is a melee weapon; spellcasting is not implemented.
