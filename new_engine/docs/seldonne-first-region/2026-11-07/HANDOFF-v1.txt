Seldonne first-region terrain/data handoff

The new private project is build/seldonne-first-region-2026-11-07/project-v1.
It is ready for the root agent's asset layout and rendered/playable review.
It has not been shown in the game or accepted as finished visual art.

The editable playable footprint is 1280 x 768 metres at world X -12288..-11008,
Z 4992..5760. At the engine's actual 3.5 m/s walk speed, the long axis takes
6.10 minutes and the diagonal 7.11 minutes before winding paths and stops.
The 1536 x 1024 metre height context uses one-metre samples and exactly meets
the original native atlas along its outermost boundary. Playable base terrain
ranges from 10.34 to 40.24 metres; the outer context blends back to the atlas
and includes higher distant ground.

layout-v1.json is the art/layout contract. Local coordinates add X -12288 and
Z 4992 to become world coordinates. The NW arrival camp is local 300,180 and
the spawn 300,188. The SW town is 320,590. Both terraces are 11.8 m high.
Their complete absolute anchors are also included in anchors_world.

Six paired perimeter arcs give a broad river around the region. The twelve
32 m full-width ribbons overlap into one union surface, approximately 52 m
across, without coplanar duplicate water triangles. Three editable tributaries
follow the west, center and eastern forest valleys. All connected reaches use
one lowland water datum at Y 8 m; this is authored slow water, not fluid
simulation. Representative main-channel widths away from tributary mouths
measure 48..53.5 m. Mouths deliberately widen into the receiving channel.

The two owned timber bridges have stable IDs 1 and 2. The southern waterfront
crossing is six metres wide; the woodland creek bridge is 3.6 metres wide.
Their generated render and collision triangles are the same geometry. Their
dry endpoints exactly meet terrain. No GPU or actual Jolt movement test has
been performed on this new region yet.

The actual production river and terrain pipeline passes the CPU audit:
58 cached terrain tiles, 56 material paint tiles, 99,294 water triangles,
3,990 wet centerline/junction probes, 109 broad cross sections, minimum tested
center water depth 1.785 m, maximum camp-to-town route grade 3.65%, and exact
Studio Save/reopen. Negative controls reject a river too narrow for the
terrain grid, a mismatched connected water datum, and a floating bridge end.

Nineteen editable ecology regions provide continuous regional groundcover,
eight denser woodland groups, open meadow, camp and town clearing. Density
estimates approximately 3,518 tree lattice candidates before actual runtime
water/slope/collision exclusions against the 7,000 tree cap. This leaves 13
ecology slots for the designer. These are candidate estimates, not measured
visible counts or a performance benchmark. Grass, ferns and near rocks use
the engine's existing distance limits and shared assets.

The remaining 16th ribbon slot is reserved for the final camp-to-town road.
The main_route controls are already graded into the base terrain and painted.
Convert that final art-directed route into a Road ribbon to clear generated
grass and trees automatically: paint alone does not suppress vegetation.

Root integration work remains: remove/replace the copied old source instance
lists and source collision, build the SW town and arrival-camp art, author
hero vegetation/rocks along the route and creek banks, relocate story actors,
and run actual renderer, movement/collision and hardware/performance checks.
The copied publisher/city metadata still describes the prior source scene;
replace that metadata during source-art integration before claiming a new
standalone experience. No engine changes are required for the data draft.

Use effective-placement-v1.height only to sample final ground for art
placement. It includes the actual production river terrain offsets and is
stored outside the project. Keep project ground/showcase.height as the
un-carved editable base so moving/removing a river restores its prior ground.

Models, textures, texture_blocks, audio, blood_assets and environments are
read-only workflow links to the accepted baseline. Never export or edit
through these links. Use a new ordinary art directory or private bank copy
for new assets. All other mutable project controls are ordinary independent
copies. The original project controls were verified byte unchanged.
