  build/seldonne-first-region-2026-11-07/project-v1
Art controls are closed for the root agent's renderer/movement review and the

Three travelers form the arrival conversation group; six more actors populate

Production input and collision contract:
  ART-MANIFEST-v1.json contains closed:true, the absolute project path, all
  placements and source rows. source_row is a1-based line number;
  source_row_text is the exact encoded line. All14-field placement rows carry
  explicit final pivotY. Manifest coordinates bind the encoded UV round-trip;
  the QA consumer still applies the engine's float/radian conversion.
  Original local publisher UCX catalog/model bindings remain unchanged for
  used town aliases. Camp aliases use the nm_city_ protected family and cook
  unchanged visual triangles. No independent collision boxes were invented.
  Houses preserve their old source floor-to-root relationship. Foundations,
  lamps and carts therefore have differing rootY; actual visual/Jolt review
  must identify and correct any inherited placement defect.

The source scene's unrelated opening.scene/opening.config, city/countryside
ground overlays and old roofs were retired from this private project. Every
replaced or removed ordinary control has an exact byte backup under:
  assets/seldonne-first-region/pre-art-controls-v1/
Exclude those backups from a distributable region add-on. The active terrain
remains the editable uncarved ground/showcase.height. Never replace that base
with effective-placement-v1.height; the latter is only an external art sampler.

Models, audio, blood_assets and environments remain read-only links to the
accepted source bank. No linked asset data or engine source was modified.
city_showcase.json and publisher_showcase.json remain enabled for the genuine
source collision/render path; their metadata now describes this new region.
