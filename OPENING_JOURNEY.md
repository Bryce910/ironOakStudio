**Legends of Valwood — A Place at the Fire**

Open [the opening chapter](/home/computer/Desktop/final_engine/Launch_Valwood_Opening.sh) and choose **Begin the Journey**. Allow about 10–15 minutes for a first playthrough.

**Windows:** [Download the standalone opening demo (13.9 GB ZIP)](/home/computer/Desktop/final_engine/new_engine/releases/Valwood-Demo-Windows-2026-11-05.zip), choose **Extract All**, then open **Valwood Demo.exe**. Keep its `bin` and `project` folders beside it. Unity is not required.

You begin as an exhausted father in his apartment. Read the bills, then follow the gold **TV / GAME CONSOLE** marker. Approach the console and press **E / A** to turn it on. Release, then press **Enter / A** to select **NEW GAME**. The transition takes you to the Meadows of Valois, beside three travelers and their cooking fire.

1. Finish conversations with **Rowan, Bram and Ellis**.
2. Speak to **Bram again** about the missing supplies. Finish his coaching to receive an old sword.
3. Follow the road toward the waystone and interact with the raider. Match his incoming attack with your guard, then strike an opening. Body hits produce blood; weapon contact produces steel sparks.
4. Win the encounter and collect the **provision satchel** beside the road.
5. Return to **Rowan**. Finish the conversation to restore health and stamina to **100** and receive **12 coins, one provision and one reputation**, once.
6. Reach the **Copper Lantern** frontage in town and speak to **Hugh**. His lead points you toward Mara at the provisions shop. Finish the conversation to complete this chapter and save the checkpoint.

Each committed cut costs **16 stamina**. A three-cut combo costs **48**, leaving **52 from a full 100** before regeneration or other actions. Use fresh clicks or centered-stick flicks during recovery; holding the stick does not repeat attacks. LT or right mouse cancels a queued cut so you can defend.

Release E/A between dialogue lines. After a menu, loading transition or loss of focus, release held controls and center the sticks. The apartment has a directed opening view; first and third person become available after Bram lends you the sword.

Defeat keeps completed conversations and the sword. Retry returns you to a safe road approach; interact with the raider again. One checkpoint saves story progress, rewards and room lights together. Loading returns you to a safe position for that progress and never awards the reward again. **Begin the Journey** replays the opening; **Continue Checkpoint** resumes earned progress. Arena coins use a separate purse.
